Exam Syllabus

Free, self-paced study companion for the CIA Part 3 exam

The 30-day study path is organized into four units that mirror the official CIA Part 3 exam blueprint. Each topic below links to its day in the interactive study path.

Unit A — Internal Audit Operations — 25% of the exam

  • Day 1: Methodologies (Std 9.3)
  • Day 2: Coordination & Reliance (Std 9.5)
  • Day 3: Financial Resource Management (Std 10.1)
  • Day 4: Human Resources Management (Std 10.2)
  • Day 5: Technological Resources (Std 10.3)
  • Day 6: Internal Audit Strategy (Std 9.2)
  • Day 7: Board & Senior Mgmt Support and Board Interaction (Std 6.3 & 8.1)
  • Day 8: Resources (Std 8.2)
  • Day 9: Building Relationships & Stakeholders (Std 11.1)

Unit B — Internal Audit Plan — 15% of the exam

  • Day 10: Understanding Governance, Risk & Control (Std 9.1)
  • Day 11: Internal Audit Plan & Audit Universe (Std 9.4)
  • Day 12: Sources of Engagements (Std 9.1)
  • Day 13: Emerging Technology & Risk (Std 9.4)

Unit C — Quality of the Internal Audit Function — 15% of the exam

  • Day 14: Quality (QAIP Overview & Reporting) (Std 8.3 & 12.3)
  • Day 15: Internal Quality Assessment (Std 12.1)
  • Day 16: External Quality Assessment (Std 8.4)
  • Day 17: Performance Measurement (Std 12.2)

Unit D — Engagement Results and Monitoring — 45% of the exam

  • Day 18: Attributes of Effective Communication (Std 11.2)
  • Day 19: Engagement Communication (Interim) (Std 13.1)
  • Day 20: Final Engagement Communication & Exit Conference (Std 15.1)
  • Day 21: Recommendations & Action Plans (Std 14.4)
  • Day 22: Risk Appetite, Tolerance & Residual Risk (Std 13.2 (I))
  • Day 23: Risk Management Process & Prioritization (Std 13.2 (II))
  • Day 24: Gathering Information & Analyzing Potential Findings (Std 14.1 & 14.2)
  • Day 25: Evaluation of Findings (Std 14.3)
  • Day 26: Communicating Results (Std 11.3)
  • Day 27: Errors and Omissions (Std 11.4)
  • Day 28: Engagement Conclusions (Std 14.5)
  • Day 29: Communicating Acceptance of Risks (Std 11.5)
  • Day 30: Confirming Implementation & Escalation (Std 15.2)
