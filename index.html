CIA Part 3 — Review

A free, self-paced 30-day study path for the CIA Part 3 exam (Certified Internal Auditor), with practice quizzes, full-length mock exams, topic review, and a quick exam-day reference. Built by GeorgeCharlie as an independent, unofficial study companion for the Global Internal Audit Standards syllabus.

The 30-day path is organized into four units that mirror the official exam blueprint: Internal Audit Operations (25% of the exam), Internal Audit Plan (15%), Quality of the Internal Audit Function (15%), and Engagement Results and Monitoring (45%). Each day covers one syllabus topic with a short lesson and an immediate practice quiz, so progress builds gradually instead of all at once.
