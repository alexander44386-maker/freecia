About

Free, self-paced study companion for the CIA Part 3 exam

What is this?

CIA Review — Part 3 is a free, independent study companion for the Certified Internal Auditor (CIA) Part 3 exam. It was built by GeorgeCharlie as a focused, distraction-free way to work through the exam syllabus.

What's inside

  • A 30-day guided study path covering all four exam sections
  • A growing bank of practice and review questions
  • Full-length mock exams with scoring and review
  • Progress tracking, mastery breakdowns, and a quick exam-day cheat sheet

Independence

This is an unofficial, independently produced study aid. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or produced by the Institute of Internal Auditors (IIA).
