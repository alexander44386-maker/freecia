Study Guide

Free, self-paced study companion for the CIA Part 3 exam

A suggested order

There are two paths through this site — pick the one that matches what you're doing:

Path 1: First time reviewing?

  • Work through 30-Day Study Mode day by day, taking each day's quiz.
  • Along the way, use Quick Quiz to test yourself on what you've unlocked so far.

Path 2: Reviewing only some topics?

  • Use Review All Topics to jump straight to the specific topics you want to revisit.
  • Use Practice Quiz to test yourself on those topics, whether or not they're unlocked.

Either way:

  • Check My Progress weekly and revisit weak topics.
  • Once the path is complete, take Mock Exams to gauge readiness.
  • In the final days before your exam, use Exam Cram for a fast review.

🗺️ 30-Day Study Mode

Your main path. It's broken into 30 short daily lessons covering all four exam sections in order, each ending with a quiz so you get immediate feedback. Start here if you haven't studied Part 3 before — it unlocks one day at a time so you build a steady habit instead of cramming.

📖 View Exam Syllabus

Lists the official exam blueprint by section and shows exactly which day(s) in the path cover each requirement — handy if you want to confirm a topic is covered or jump straight to it. See the full Exam Syllabus page.

⚡ Quick Quiz

Found inside the Study Path screen. It's 10 random questions, but only from the topics you've already unlocked, so it's a fast way to check your grasp of what you've studied so far without spoiling material you haven't reached yet.

📚 Review All Topics

The same 30 days, but unlocked and searchable all at once. Use this once you've made some progress on the path and want to jump straight to a specific topic to reinforce or re-read, rather than going in order.

⚡ Practice Quiz

10 random questions from across all 30 days, not just the ones you've unlocked, so you can pick any unit (A–D) or all of them and test yourself regardless of where you are in the path. Good for a quick daily check-in, and it feeds your mastery stats on the Progress screen.

📝 Mock Exam

Four full-length, timed practice exams that mirror the real test experience. Save these for later in your prep, after you've been through the path at least once, so the score is a meaningful measure of readiness rather than a guess.

📋 Exam Cram

A dense, tabbed reference covering every unit's key facts, all on one screen. Not meant for first-time learning — it's built for the final few days before the exam, when you just need a fast refresher.

🎯 Exam Tips

Covers exam-day strategy: pacing and time management, how to weight your prep across sections, how to read tricky questions, common traps to avoid, and a final-days checklist. See the full Exam Tips page.

📊 My Progress

Your dashboard: days completed, mock exam scores, mastery by section (A–D), your top weak topics, and a daily accuracy chart. Check this regularly to decide where to focus next — it's the best way to know if you're ready.
