Exam Tips

Free, self-paced study companion for the CIA Part 3 exam

Strategy for exam day itself — pairs well with Exam Cram for final review.

⏱️ Pacing & Time Management

  • 100 questions in 120 minutes ≈ 72 seconds per question — don't let any single question eat your buffer.
  • Do a first pass answering everything you're confident about; flag anything uncertain and move on.
  • Check the clock around question 25, 50, and 75 — you should be roughly a quarter, half, and three-quarters through.
  • Save remaining time at the end for flagged questions, not for re-reading everything from scratch.

📊 Weight Your Prep by Domain

  • Section A — IA Function & Strategy: 25%
  • Section B — Audit Planning & Coordination: 15%
  • Section C — Quality Assurance (QAIP): 15%
  • Section D — Engagement Results & Communication: 45%

Section D alone is nearly half the exam — make sure findings, root cause analysis, recommendations, and communication attributes are rock-solid before test day.

🔍 How to Read the Question

  • Look for the "most correct" or "best" answer — several choices may be technically true, but only one fully satisfies the standard.
  • Answer according to the Global Internal Audit Standards, not on how your own organization actually operates.
  • Watch for absolute language ("always," "never," "immediately terminate") — these choices are often wrong or too extreme.
  • Identify who is being asked about — CAE, senior management, or the board — since responsibilities differ for each.

⚠️ Common Traps

  • Confusing who is responsible for an action versus who should merely be informed.
  • Mixing up interim vs. final communication requirements.
  • Picking an answer that's factually true but doesn't actually answer what the question asked.
  • Overanalyzing and talking yourself out of a well-studied first instinct.

✅ Final-Days Checklist

  • Work through Exam Cram tab by tab, saving Section D for last since it carries the most weight.
  • Take at least one full Mock Exam under timed conditions to build stamina and practice pacing.
  • Revisit your weak topics on the Progress screen instead of re-reviewing everything equally.
  • The night before: light review only. Cramming brand-new material rarely helps recall.
  • On exam day: arrive early, bring required ID, and expect a short tutorial/NDA screen before the timed portion starts.
